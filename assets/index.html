Job Board

Settings

Welcome. The board starts out showing remote jobs only. Tell it which roles you are looking for and where you can work from, and it will only bring you those. You can change any of this at any time.

The roles you want. Separate with commas or new lines. A job is shown when its title contains every word of one entry, in any order: “system engineer” also finds “Senior Systems Engineer”. Leave empty to see every role.

Only for titles you do not want. A job is hidden when its title contains one of these, even if it also matches the list above.

A job is hidden when its title or description contains one of these phrases: the same words in the same order, as whole words (add a plural as its own entry). Capitals and punctuation do not matter. A mention that says it does not apply, such as “Security clearance: None” or “no security clearance required”, is not counted.

Only cards still in New, with no notes or files, are hidden. Nothing is deleted: take an entry off a list and its cards come back. Open one and change its column to keep it.

    Many remote jobs are limited to certain countries. List every way yours is written. Jobs open worldwide, or with no country stated, are always kept. Leave empty for anywhere.

    Kinds of job to show

    The board starts out remote-only. A job counts as remote only when its listing plainly says so and nothing in the posting says otherwise. Tick the others to see those jobs too; each card then says which kind it is. Changing this applies from the next check, which takes longer the more kinds are ticked. Cards already on the board stay.

    A hybrid or on-site job is shown only when its location names one of these: a city, a state, or however your area is written. Remote jobs are not held to this. Leave empty for anywhere within “Where I can work from”.

    On: a job is shown only when its employer runs a neurodiversity hiring program, is on the disability inclusion index, posted it on an autism job board, or says so in the posting. Off: every job that matches the settings above is shown, and jobs from those employers keep their label. Changing this applies from the next check; cards already on the board stay.

    Employers and files

    The employers whose career sites are checked are listed in a text file you can edit. Changes are picked up on the next check.
